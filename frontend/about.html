About ApexRX_Smart

ApexRX_Smart is a digital healthcare platform built in Kigali. It shows where medicines are available, so that patients and the professionals who care for them can find a medicine quickly and reliably, close to home.

Behind every search, pharmacies keep their stock and prices up to date and source medicines from verified suppliers. ApexRX connects these links of the medicine chain without replacing the systems they already use.

How it works
— Why it matters

Finding a medicine should not be a guessing game

Medicine availability is often hard to know. Patients, healthcare professionals, pharmacies and suppliers face delays, uncertainty and missed opportunities because information about where medicines are, and how to get them, is limited and scattered.

Our story

ApexRX_Smart started from two questions asked every day in Kigali. Patients and doctors ask: where can this medicine be found today? Pharmacies ask: which supplier has it in stock, and at what price? Today, pharmacists often go from depot to depot to find out, losing time and money. ApexRX shows suppliers’ stock and prices in real time, so a pharmacy knows which depot to buy from before it travels. We are building this with pharmacies and suppliers, one district at a time.

Where we work

We start in Kigali, across Gasabo, Kicukiro and Nyarugenge, with the goal of serving the whole of Rwanda.
