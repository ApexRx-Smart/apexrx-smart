— Who we are

About ApexRX_Smart

ApexRX_Smart is a digital healthcare platform that connects patients, pharmacies and suppliers in Kigali. It shows which pharmacies have a medicine in stock, so people can find what they need before they leave home.

For pharmacies, it is a simple workspace to keep stock up to date, compare supplier prices and order. For suppliers, it is a direct line to verified pharmacies that need their products.

How it works
— Why ApexRX

One search instead of many trips

Patients

Today, finding a medicine can mean going from one pharmacy to the next. ApexRX shows nearby pharmacies that have it, with the price and how recently the stock was confirmed.

Pharmacies

Pharmacies make their stock visible to patients, track batches and expiry dates, and compare prices from several suppliers in one place.

Suppliers

Suppliers receive price requests from verified pharmacies, send offers and follow each order, instead of handling everything by phone.

— Our mission

Our Mission

To make medicine availability visible and reliable, so that patients find their medicines faster and pharmacies and suppliers can serve them better.

— Our vision

Our Vision

A Rwanda where no one has to search pharmacy after pharmacy for a medicine, and where every pharmacy can plan its stock with confidence.

— What guides us

Our values

Trust

Pharmacies and suppliers are checked by our team, using their licence details, before they can trade on the platform.

Privacy

Patients search without an account. They see whether a medicine is available, never a pharmacy’s exact quantities.

Accuracy

Every result shows when the stock was last confirmed, so people know how fresh the information is.

Access for everyone

The platform works on a phone and is available in Kinyarwanda, English and French.
